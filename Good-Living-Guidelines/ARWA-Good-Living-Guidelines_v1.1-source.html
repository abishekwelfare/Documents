Good Living Document

The Owners shall also abide by rules and regulations prescribed in the Good Living document. Any violations of the Good Living Document will be subject to penalties as decided by the MC. The MC reserves the right to review and revise the Good Living Document based on emergency and such revisions / amendments shall be ratified in the next AGM / EGM.

  1. Every Owner who lets his/her/their apartment for occupation by others on lease, tenancy, mortgagee, or otherwise, shall include in the relevant Agreement, binding the occupant to pay, within due date, the monthly maintenance assessments directly to the Association, failing which the penalties may be imposed. A copy of the said Agreement, along with an undertaking by the occupant to abide strictly by the Bye-laws of the Association, and to make payment in full, and in time, all maintenance assessment as raised, shall be submitted to the Association before occupation of the Apartment. This, however, shall not absolve the Owner from his/her/their responsibility to ensure that all assessments on his/her/their Apartment are paid in time and in the event of any default by his/her/their occupant, shall himself/herself/themselves make all payment as raised by the MC.
  2. Every Owner / Resident shall provide a copy of their ID proof ( Aadhar / Voter ID / Passport ) along with details of family members to ARWA at the time of occupation of the flat to facilitate updation of residents records.
  3. Every Owner shall perform promptly all maintenance and repair work within his/her/their Apartment, which if omitted would affect the building in entirety, or in a part belonging to other owners being expressly responsible for the damages and liabilities that his/her/their failure to do so may endanger. In doing so he/she/they shall not make any alteration, or modification which may affect the facade or the main structure of the building or the common walls or floors between two units.
  4. Every Owner shall bear the cost of all repairs to the internal installations of his/her/their Apartments.
  5. Every Owner/Resident shall fully, and without delay, reimburse the Association for any expenditure in repairing or replacing any damages to the Common Areas and Facilities caused through his/her/their fault.
  6. Every Owner/Resident shall grant the right of entry to the MC members, or any person authorized by them, in case of any emergency originating in or threatening his/her/their Apartment whether the Owner is present or not.
  7. Every Owner/Resident shall permit the MC members or any person authorized by them to enter the Apartment for the purpose of performing installations, alterations, or repairs to the mechanical or electrical services, provided that the requests for entry are made in advance, and that such entry is at a time convenient to the Owner. In case of an emergency such entry shall be immediate and without notice.
  8. Every Owner/Resident shall ensure that the Apartment is not used for any purpose other than residential. The Apartments shall also not be used as serviced apartments.
  9. Every Owner / Resident shall draw metro water only from the outlets designed by the builder and shall be used for cooking and drinking purpose only.
  10. Every Owner / Resident shall ensure that while move in / move out, heavy vehicles / truck shall be parked near maintenance office room. Loading / unloading of articles is allowed between 6 AM to 8 PM.
  11. Every Owner/Resident shall ensure that his/her/their children play only at places allotted if any and during the hours prescribed by the MC. Hard games like football, basketball, cricket, etc. are not allowed in the Complex since no infrastructures for such games were provided by the Builder.
  12. Every Owner/Resident shall ensure that the building and the Common Areas should not be made dirty and that garbage or trash is segregated and disposed all in accordance with the rules and regulations prescribed by the Association from time to time.
  13. Every Owner/Resident shall ensure that the rights and privileges of other owners are respected and that no inconvenience is caused to them in any manner.
  14. Every Owner/Resident shall ensure that the staff employed by him/her/them bear a good character and shall be responsible for their behavior and actions while in his/her/their service. Owners/Resident shall be responsible to ensure that their staff follow all rules laid by the MC, without exception.
  15. Every Owner/Resident shall use the lifts in such a manner as not to damage them in any way whatsoever and follow the rules and regulations prescribed by Association from time to time.
  16. Every Owner/Resident shall exercise due care in ensuring that the noise/audio levels whilst using musical instruments, television sets, amplifiers etc. or whilst undertaking carpentry, civil work etc., shall not cause any undue disturbance to neighbors and/or residents of other apartments and shall follow the rules and regulations prescribed by Association from time to time.
  17. Residents keeping domestic animals or other pets shall abide by the rules and regulations prescribed by the Association from time to time. Pet owners shall not cause any inconvenience to other residents especially while moving out pets for natural call / exercise. “Guidelines on pet and street dogs” issued by The Animal Welfare Board of India from time to time shall be adhered. The detailed Pet Rules and Coexistence Guidelines set out in Annexure A of this document form part of these rules and shall be followed by all pet owners and residents.
  18. Every Owner shall furnish relevant particulars of any person/persons other than the Owners themselves in occupation of his/her/their Apartment as may be required by the MC. A letter of authorization for such occupation shall be given to the MC before the occupation.
  19. No Owner shall sell, or otherwise transfer his/her/their Apartment to anyone without notice to the Association and/or without paying in full all amounts due to the Association. Any default in this regard will result in the transferee being denied any or all of the services rendered by the Association including the supply of water unless the transferee undertakes to pay all the dues and does so before occupation of the Apartment.
  20. No Owner shall make any structural or other modifications which may alter the facade of the building in any way, whatsoever, save and except window grills installed from inside of the Apartment, which may be provided as a measure of safety, but only as approved by the MC, and under its written orders.
  21. No Owner/Resident shall make any structural modification whatsoever within the Apartment which shall compromise the structural safety of the building such as demolition or modification to load bearing columns/walls etc. or repositioning of toilets/bathrooms from the original/approved plans etc. Any non-structural modification or alteration or repair (other than normal wear and tear related) within the Apartment or on installations located therein shall need prior written approval of the MC. Such requests by the Owner shall be in writing to the President/Secretary of the MC. The MC shall have the obligation to communicate its decision within 15 days of receipt of the request. The MC may even demand a written report/opinion of a Structural Engineering firm, chosen from reputed firms, to be arranged by such Owner at his/her cost. All decisions taken by MC in this regard shall be final and binding.
  22. No Owner/Resident shall place or cause to be placed in the lobbies, vestibules, stairways, elevators and other Common areas any furniture, potted plants, packages, chappals, shoes or objects of any kind, except while in normal transit through them.
  23. Residents should park their cars in the allotted parking slots as per builder’s drawing plan. Visitors should park their cars in allotted slots in the exit gate ramp. When visitors parking area is not available, the visitors should park the cars outside the premises and not to park anywhere inside the complex. Visitors parking is available free of charge between 6 am to 10 pm. Appropriate parking charges will be collected for such parking beyond 10 PM. Resident whose visitor stays overnight and parks his/her car in the visitors’ parking slot, should inform the security and enter in the register the name of the visitor, resident’s name and block and flat number. The charges for overnight car parking fixed by the Association to be paid by the resident/visitor at the time of removing the vehicle. It is a specific pre-condition that all vehicles parked within ARWA Complex are at the owner’s risk.
  24. Residents should park their two wheelers (Bike, scooter, bi-cycles etc.) in their respective car parking area only. In other words, such two wheelers are not allowed to be parked in the ARWA common area and entrance area nor in other residents’ car parking area without their permission.
  25. No Owner/Resident shall place any objects in the Common Areas, especially in the lobbies, window sills, lifts, stairways, emergency exits etc. and shall follow all rules and regulations prescribed for this purpose, by the Association from time to time.
  26. No Owner/Resident shall put up any hoarding, advertisement, notice, or poster of any kind, in or on ARWA Complex, except as authorized by the Association.
  27. No Owner/Resident shall install wiring for electrical, telephone, or fax machines, television antennae, air-conditioning units, or machines on the exterior of the building, which protrudes through, or above the walls or roof, except as authorized by the MC.
  28. No Owner/Resident, or any person connected with him/her/them, shall cause any damage, whatsoever, to any asset of the Association. In the event of so doing, the loss on account of damage will be borne by the Owner/Resident. The assessment of loss by the MC shall be final and binding.
  29. Residents shall adhere as to not to store explosives or dangerous or any objectionable materials in the flats.
  30. Owner / Resident should ensure that the passage way should be kept free of obstruction to facilitate free movement of residents. The residents are strictly prohibited to dispose of garbage or any other waste material through windows of flats including bathrooms that creates nuisance to other residents/cars parked in the areas beneath at respective parking areas or to the common area and people moving thereon.
  31. No Owner/Resident shall use any part of the premises including his/her/their Apartment for any commercial activities whatsoever. Usage of apartment premises for the purpose of service apartment / company guest houses (where an owner leases out the apartment to a company which provides the apartment for temporary stay without a lease/rental agreement OR any usage promoting transitional stay) is prohibited by the Association. Upon violations of above conditions, MC reserves the right to restrict usage of common facilities.
  32. Owner/Resident should ensure that all safety norms stipulated and required to be followed by the residents and their guests are followed. The Association has no liability for any unfortunate mishaps inside the premises. The residents should ensure that their guests also follow the safety and other rules.
  33. No Owner/Resident shall object to any work being undertaken by the MC which is in the common interest of the Owners, even if such work may cause some inconvenience to him/her/them.
  34. No Owner/Resident shall, under any circumstances, threaten, abuse, reprimand, assault or in any way take up with the staff employed by the Association, but may report any misbehaviour, or neglect of duty by them to the MC.
  35. No owner / Resident shall engage ARWA staffs for personal use.
  36. All Owner/Resident of ARWA Complex shall ensure that their vehicles are driven within prescribed speed limits keeping utmost safety of Residents in mind and shall also follow all rules and regulations prescribed for this purpose, by the Association from time to time.
  37. No Owner/family member(s) or the tenant or any other person(s) shall form any parallel society/association.
  38. Any Owner/Resident violating any rules and regulations/Bye-laws of the Association may invite disciplinary/legal action against him/her by the Association so as to make them comply with the said rules and regulations.

Annexure A – Pet Rules and Coexistence Guidelines

This Annexure forms part of the Good Living Document and supplements Rule 17.

Part A – Rules for Pet Owners (mandatory)

Registration and documents

  1. All pets must be registered with the Association.
  2. Owners must submit the following details to the Association at the time of registration: pet photograph; latest vaccination records; GCC licence details; microchip details (as per GCC); and owner contact details.
  3. All pets must be vaccinated as per GCC rules.

Control in common areas

  1. Pets must always be on a leash in all common areas.
  2. Pets must not roam freely inside the apartment complex.
  3. Children below 15 years of age should not walk large dogs.
  4. Pets must be accompanied by a responsible adult handler at all times.
  5. Pets should not be walked in the children's play area.
  6. Pets known to be active or aggressive may be required to wear a muzzle in common areas.
  7. Pets must not be left unattended in common areas.
  8. Pet owners must ensure pets do not chase vehicles, cyclists or pedestrians.

Cleanliness

  1. Pet owners must pick up pet waste immediately.
  2. Owners must carry poop bags on every walk.
  3. Pet waste must be disposed of only in the designated bins.
  4. Urine in lifts, corridors, staircases, parking areas and entrances must be cleaned immediately by the pet owner.
  5. Repeated failure to clean up after pets may attract penalties.

Responsibility

  1. Owners are fully responsible for any injury or damage caused by their pets.

Feeding community dogs / cats

  1. Residents feeding community dogs or cats should do so only at designated locations approved by the Association.
  2. Feeding points should not obstruct walkways or entrances.
  3. Feeding must be done in a clean and hygienic manner.

Penalties and enforcement

Serious incidents involving aggression or safety risks may result in disciplinary action under applicable laws.

Pet Walker Code of Conduct (suggested)

  • Carry a leash, waste bags, a water bottle and cleaning tissue.
  • Keep pets under control at all times.
  • Avoid crowded times where possible.
  • Respect residents who are fearful of animals.
  • Clean up immediately after the pet.
  • Be friendly and responsible.
  • Please do not take your pet into the lift if other residents are already inside.
  • Please use a body harness for large pets for better control.

Part B – Guidelines for All Residents

While all pet owners are expected to follow the rules in Part A, the following simple practices help everyone live together happily:

  1. Treat pets as you would people who cannot talk.
  2. Pets have emotions and are sensitive to how we treat them.
  3. They have fears and worries just as we do, so let us respect them.
  4. Do not tease, provoke, chase or deliberately frighten pets.
  5. Do not touch, pet or feed a pet without the owner's permission.
  6. Parents should teach children how to behave safely around animals.
  7. Residents who are uncomfortable around pets should raise it politely rather than confront aggressively.
  8. Give pets and their handlers reasonable space in corridors, lifts and walkways.
  9. Avoid crowding a pet, especially in lifts.
  10. Do not block designated pet walking routes or relief areas.
  11. Use common areas in a way that does not deliberately interfere with pet exercise.
  12. Please avoid running, screaming or waving objects near pets.
  13. Parents remain responsible for supervising children in areas where pets are present.
  14. If someone is fearful of animals, both sides should cooperate respectfully to make space.
  15. No resident may prevent a pet owner from using the common lifts.

Golden Rules for Coexistence

  1. Pet owners control their pets.
  2. Non-pet owners respect responsible pet ownership.
  3. Children are supervised around animals.
  4. Common areas belong to everyone.
  5. Cleanliness and safety are non-negotiable.
  6. Disagreements are resolved through dialogue, not confrontation.

Let us work together towards a better and happier community.
