Background

The AGM held on 02 August 2026 approved an increase in the maintenance charge from ₹3.25 to ₹4.25 per sq.ft. per month, effective from the Oct–Dec 2026 quarter. Select your flat below to see your revised quarterly charge. Flats with a monthly maintenance above ₹7,500 also attract 18% GST, as required by law. If you pay monthly rather than quarterly, see the payment guide below your total — the quarterly figure sometimes doesn't split into three equal rupee amounts, so one or two months carry ₹1 extra to make up the difference.
